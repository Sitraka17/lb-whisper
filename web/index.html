lb-whisper, in your browser

Luxembourgish speech recognition · Whisper large-v3-turbo fine-tuned on FLEURS · Sitraka Forler

Abstract. Record yourself or drop an audio file: the transcript is computed on this computer, with the GPU of your browser. The audio is never uploaded anywhere; only the model weights are downloaded, once, and then kept in the browser cache.1 Trained on 8.5 h of read speech, so expect mistakes on spontaneous speech, names and noisy rooms.2

Any length; long recordings are cut into 30-second windows. Speak Luxembourgish; French or German passages will come out in Luxembourgish spelling or be skipped.
